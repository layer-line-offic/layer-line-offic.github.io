Before you print

Which files do I get?

Each design includes an STL for slicing and, where listed, a STEP file for editing in your own CAD software. The listing shows which formats that design ships with.

Which printers will it work on?

Any FDM printer with a build plate large enough for the part. Each listing gives the layer height, nozzle size, material and support settings the design was drawn for.

Parts fit too tight or too loose. What do I do?

Clearances are set for a 0.4 mm nozzle at 0.20 mm layers. If your printer runs tight, scale the moving part by 101 to 102 percent in your slicer, or change the clearance in the STEP file.

Can I print in a different material?

Yes. The listed material is the one the design was drawn for. Stiffer or more flexible filaments change how snap fits and hinges behave, so test a small section first.

Can I get a refund?

Digital files cannot be returned, but a file that does not open or does not match its listing is fixed or refunded. See the refund policy.
