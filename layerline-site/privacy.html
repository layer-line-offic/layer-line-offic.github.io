Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

What this site stores

This site does not use accounts, does not set tracking cookies and does not run advertising. Browsing the catalog does not send your details to us.

When you buy

You pay on a page run by our payment provider. They collect your name, email and payment details to process the order and send your files. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can support you. We never see your card or UPI details.

When you write to us

If you email us, we keep the message and your address to reply and to keep a record of your order or request.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the payment and file-delivery providers needed to complete your order, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you by writing to layerline@yahoo.com.
