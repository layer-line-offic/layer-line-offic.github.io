Refunds

Last updated 1 November 2026

Digital files

Because files are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind.

When we will fix it or refund you

  • The file will not download or will not open.
  • The file does not match its listing, for example the wrong model or missing formats.
  • You were charged twice for the same design.

How to ask

Email layerline@yahoo.com within 14 days of buying, with your order email and the name of the design. We reply within two working days. If we cannot fix the problem, we refund the purchase to the original payment method.

Print problems

If a print fails, write to us before asking for a refund. Most fit and settings problems are solved with a small change to scale, temperature or supports, and we are glad to help.
