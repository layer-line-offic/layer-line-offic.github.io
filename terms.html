License and terms

Last updated 1 November 2026

What you are buying

You are buying a license to use a digital STL file. You do not buy the copyright in the design. The designer keeps it. Every design is sold with two license options, Personal and Commercial, and you choose one at the buy button.

Personal license

With the Personal license you may:

  • Print the design for yourself, as many times as you like.
  • Give printed copies away as gifts.
  • Modify the STL for your own use, for example to scale or adjust it.

Commercial license

The Commercial license includes everything in the Personal license. It also lets you sell the physical prints you make from the design, in any quantity, online or in person. One Commercial license covers one person or one business.

What is not allowed with either license

  • Selling, sharing, uploading or reselling the STL files, modified or not.
  • Selling printed copies with a Personal license.
  • Claiming the design as your own work.
  • Passing your license on to another person or business.

Print at your own risk

Designs are tested on the settings shown on each listing. Results depend on your printer, material and slicer. Parts that carry load, heat, food or people must be tested by you for that use. Files are provided as is, without any warranty of fitness for a particular purpose.

Payment

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider on its own page. Prices are shown in US dollars. Taxes such as VAT or sales tax are added at checkout where they apply.
